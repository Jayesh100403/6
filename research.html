M.Sc. thesis · NCPRE, IIT Bombay

Simulation and Optical Design of Multilayer Transparent Conducting Oxides

I investigated IZO/Ag/IZO oxide–metal–oxide structures as transparent electrodes for photovoltaic devices, with the emphasis gradually shifting toward photonics and the physical interpretation of light propagation through nanoscale multilayers.

01

Built a SETFOS and Python workflow for transmission, reflection, absorption, photon-flux and theoretical current calculations.

02

Examined non-bulk optical behaviour in sub-10 nm silver using Drude-based interpretation, LSPR considerations and realistic film-growth constraints.

03

Treated strong simulated gains as theoretical design limits, explicitly separating them from the lower improvements expected after fabrication.

04

Compared optical performance with electrical requirements through sheet resistance and Haacke figure-of-merit analysis.
